Social Habitat

Two groups of four creatures, each group in its own copy of the same world. Every creature learns food values from pixels, plans routes, and learns its own movement. Switch each group's abilities on or off to see what they add. Learning from others: watching another creature eat teaches the watcher that food's value without tasting it. Neural movement: a small network predicts where a button press lands from the surrounding terrain, instead of simple counts. Winter planning: for 500 steps of every 2,400 no food grows; creatures sense temperature, learn which temperatures bring famine and forecast it 200 steps ahead, and hibernate to save energy. Buttons are rewired at step 7,800. Movement version · Pixel version
