Learning Habitat

Pixel version · Two creatures live in identical worlds. Each must eat to keep its energy up. Food has a colour and a shape, and the season decides which foods nourish and which poison. Before every meal a creature predicts the result, then learns from its surprise. The simple creature keeps one set of beliefs. The lifelike creature also remembers past seasons, recognises when one returns, learns faster when surprised, and sleeps to replay its experiences.
