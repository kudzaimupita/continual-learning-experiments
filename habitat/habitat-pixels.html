Pixel Habitat

The creatures no longer get told a food's colour and shape. Each food is a small noisy 7×7 image, and each creature learns with a tiny neural network that turns pixels into a predicted energy value. It has to discover for itself what matters. From step 6,600 a purple food appears that none has ever seen. The third creature also plans: it imagines routes through up to three meals and walks around food it predicts is poisonous, since stepping on any food means eating it. The label version is here.
